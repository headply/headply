## Hi, I'm Mayowa

AI engineer. I build the data side of AI systems: getting data out of sources that don't offer an API, turning it into clean, reliable pipelines, and building the tools and checks that put it to work.

**What I work on**

- **Data extraction.** Production scrapers and APIs for platforms without one. My public Actors on [Apify](https://apify.com/headply) cover Google Trends, Yelp, Airbnb and Vrbo, Threads, Jumia, Capterra reviews, and YouTube and TikTok transcripts.
- **Data pipelines and datasets.** Point-in-time fundamentals from SEC XBRL filings, daily-refreshed job market data, long-run ESG controversy panels.
- **AI systems.** Retrieval and semantic search, MCP servers that give models real tools, and evaluation harnesses that show whether any of it works.
- **Geospatial.** Deforestation screening of supplier plots for EUDR compliance.
